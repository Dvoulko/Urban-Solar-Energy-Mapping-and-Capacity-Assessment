# Rooftop Solar Potential Analysis using QGIS
### Assessing Urban PV Capacity for Green Transition in Thessaloniki's City Center

---

## 🇬🇧 English Version

### Description
This project focuses on the spatial analysis and calculation of rooftop surface areas within an urban block in the city center of Thessaloniki, Greece, using **QGIS**. The primary objective is to evaluate the solar energy potential and estimate the photovoltaic (PV) installation capacity on existing buildings, supporting the urban green transition and the adoption of Renewable Energy Sources (RES).

### Software & Tools
*   **GIS Software:** QGIS
*   **Methodology:** Spatial Surface Area Calculation / Urban Solar Resource Assessment
*   **Focus Areas:** Renewable Energy Sources (RES), Urban Sustainability, Photovoltaic Potential
